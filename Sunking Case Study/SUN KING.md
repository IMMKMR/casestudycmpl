# **SUN KING × COVANA**

## **THE CHALLENGE**

Sun King wanted to build a stronger digital presence, engage rural audiences and drive awareness of its solar-powered solutions.

### **THE COVANA APPROACH**

We built Sun King's social presence from the ground up, combining **brand positioning, a clear messaging platform, localized content, community engagement and performance marketing** to turn its channels into a space for discovery, engagement and product conversations.

---

# **01 / THE MESSAGE**

## **SET THE SCENE WITH SUN KING**

We moved communication beyond showcasing products to showing the moments, experiences and benefits they could enable.

The platform became the foundation for Sun King's creative communication — from “Reliable Power in Your Hands” and “Lighting India, Seamlessly” to “Experience Sunlight Every Time\!” and “Light That Uplifts You.”

---

# **02 / THE CONTENT STRATEGY**

We built the content engine around three key content approaches:

### **LOCALIZED REELS & CREATIVES**

Content adapted to the audience and regional context, helping Sun King feel closer to the people it wanted to reach.

### **PRODUCT EDUCATION THROUGH STORYTELLING**

Products were introduced through stories and content that made their benefits easier to understand.

### **REAL-USER TESTIMONIALS & DEMOS**

Real experiences and demonstrations brought Sun King's solar-powered solutions to life.

---

# **03 / THE GROWTH ENGINE**

### **COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT**

We leveraged **local influencers** and developed a **region-specific engagement strategy** to build stronger audience interaction.

### **PERFORMANCE MARKETING**

Targeted media spends were used to **boost reach and grow followers**, supported by festival- and offer-led campaigns.

### **THE GROWTH LOOP**

**Relevant content → More reach → More engagement → More people discovering Sun King → More product conversations**

---

# **04 / THE BRAND FILM**

## **TAKING “SET THE SCENE” TO THE BIG SCREEN.**

To take Sun King's story beyond social, **Covana conceptualised and executed a celebrity-led TVC end-to-end — from concept and scripting to production — with Rajkummar Rao.**

The film brought the brand's promise into **everyday life**, showing how Sun King lighting can create moments of **warmth, safety and progress**.

### **COVANA OWNED THE ENTIRE JOURNEY**

**CONCEPT**  
 → **SCRIPTING**  
 → **CELEBRITY**  
 → **PRODUCTION**  
 → **FINAL FILM**

### **WHY IT MATTERED**

**RAJKUMMAR RAO × SUN KING**

A recognisable Indian celebrity brought **star power and relatability** to the brand, helping make Sun King's story more memorable and emotionally resonant.
