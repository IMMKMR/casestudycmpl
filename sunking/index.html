The Challenge

Sun King wanted to build a stronger digital presence, engage rural audiences and drive awareness of its solar-powered solutions. We built Sun King's social presence from the ground up, combining brand positioning, a clear messaging platform, localized content, community engagement and performance marketing to turn its channels into a space for discovery, engagement and product conversations.

01 / The Message

Set the Scene with Sun King

We moved communication beyond showcasing products to showing the moments, experiences and benefits they could enable. The platform became the foundation for Sun King's creative communication — from "Reliable Power in Your Hands" and "Lighting India, Seamlessly" to "Experience Sunlight Every Time!" and "Light That Uplifts You."

02 / The Content Strategy

Building the Content Engine

We built the content engine around three key content approaches: Localized Reels & Creatives, Product Education through Storytelling, and Real-User Testimonials & Demos.

03 / The Growth Engine

Building Stronger Interaction

We leveraged local influencers and developed a region-specific engagement strategy to build stronger audience interaction. Targeted media spends were used to boost reach and grow followers, supported by festival- and offer-led campaigns.

Relevant content → More reach → More engagement → More people discovering Sun King → More product conversations

Brand Identity

Logo Construction & Use

The logo features two elements: a wordmark and the lion symbol in a strict horizontal lock-up. A core principle of the identity is "Let It Breathe"—the logo is always protected by an area of clear space (equivalent to one letter 's') that must remain clear of all other text and graphic elements.

Primary Palette

The Sun King™ yellow (Pantone 108 C) represents the shining sun in all its majesty, brilliance and warmth. It is the colour of optimism and conveys a compelling sense of youthfulness and energy.

Color Palette & Typography

Our brand visual language is built on distinct, consistent elements that create an emotional connection with our audience.

Secondary: White

Used for clean, uncluttered backgrounds allowing the primary yellow to shine.

Secondary: Black

A rich black (R:35 G:31 B:32) used for primary typography and strong contrast elements.

Secondary: Grey

A soft grey (R:147 G:149 B:152) used sparingly to support communication hierarchy.

Primary Font: Akzidenz Grotesk

Akzidenz Grotesk is used for all copy from headlines to main content. All headlines must be in uppercase, whereas body copy must appear in lowercase.
