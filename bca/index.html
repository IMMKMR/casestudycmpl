Brand Overview

Founded in 1977 as Bharat Insecticides, Bharat Certis AgriScience Ltd. (BCA) has grown from humble beginnings into a leading authority in sustainable crop protection. Following a transformative joint venture with Japanese corporations Mitsui & Co., Ltd. and Nippon Soda Co., Ltd., BCA expanded its operations into over 30 countries with two state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities.

Problem Statement

As the enterprise scaled internationally, its historical domestic identity did not communicate world-class scientific authority or Japanese R&D synergy. The challenge was twofold: projecting international agronomic credibility to overseas OEM partners while simultaneously reassuring existing Indian farming communities of their lasting dependability and local care.

Strategic Objectives

Develop an empathetic brand architecture centered around dual archetypes: The Caregiver and The Creator. Create an evergreen symbol pairing an upward arrow of progress with an agriscience leaf motif. Establish an adaptable, digital-first design vocabulary utilizing Royal Trust Blue and Sustainable Growth Green across global divisions.

Unity in Vision, Strength in Innovation

To celebrate the global joint venture with Japanese champions Mitsui & Co., Ltd. and Nippon Soda Co., Ltd., COVANA crafted an evergreen Key Visual architecture. The overlapping blue and green structural rings represent operational synergy—uniting trusted agricultural traditions with robotic, laboratory-grade agriscience precision.

Brand Evolution & Architecture

Transitioning from a legacy-driven visual identity (established in 1977 as Bharat Insecticides) to a modern, progressive global agriscience authority following the strategic joint venture with Mitsui & Co., Ltd. and Nippon Soda Co., Ltd.

Before (Est. 1977)

The Legacy Domestic Identity

Originally established in 1977 as Bharat Insecticides, the company spent decades cultivating deep trust within Indian farming communities. However, as global capabilities expanded, the existing heritage logo no longer conveyed modern scientific leadership or world-class manufacturing scale.

After (New Global Identity) • Pure Vector

The Global Agriscience Authority

Following the strategic joint venture with Japanese champions Mitsui & Co., Ltd. and Nippon Soda Co., Ltd., BCA evolved into a progressive international powerhouse. The refreshed emblem presents a sharper, future-ready vector architecture that balances cutting-edge innovation with enduring grassroots dependability.

1 B & C — Smooth & Interconnected

The smooth, rounded, and intertwined letterforms of 'B' and 'C' symbolize collaboration, operational harmony, and the interconnected ecosystem of agriculture, community, and scientific precision.

2 A & Leaf — Progress & Nurture

The sharp upward arrow of the 'A' represents dynamic growth, technological progress, and a forward-thinking mindset. Embedded directly inside its negative space is a crisp agricultural leaf motif, symbolizing sustainable farming practices and nurturing environmental care.

3 Positioning: Caregiver + Creator

We harmonized two distinct archetypes: The Caregiver (deep local community understanding, farmer well-being, and dependability) with The Creator (cutting-edge Japanese agriscience innovation and future-focused problem solving).

FAQs

Find quick answers about COVANA Studio's strategic brand positioning, architecture, and design methodologies for BCA.

Why did BCA initiate a brand redesign following the joint venture?
+
Following the strategic joint venture with Mitsui & Co., Ltd. and Nippon Soda Co., Ltd., BCA expanded its capabilities across 30+ international markets. Their legacy visual identity required an evolution to project global Japanese R&D excellence while safeguarding decades of earned domestic farmer trust.
How does the logo geometry encode agriscience values?
+
COVANA engineered a fluid monogram where interconnected letters 'B' and 'C' represent community collaboration and natural symbiosis. The upward-facing trajectory of 'A' forms an arrow of growth, enclosing an agriscience leaf motif within its negative space.
What typography and color philosophy was deployed?
+
The brand utilizes Zona Pro across all applications for its legible clarity and approachable modernity. The color palette unites Royal Trust Blue (Pantone 2145 C / C100 M65), representing structural dependability, with Sustainable Growth Green (Pantone 3501 C / C70 Y100), representing agricultural vitality.
What was the full scope of deliverables produced by COVANA?
+
COVANA formulated the full strategic brand positioning, corporate brand guidelines, executive stationery suites (letterheads, visiting cards, packaging), employee identification badging, enterprise HTML email signatures, PowerPoint presentation architecture, and a phased rollout plan.
